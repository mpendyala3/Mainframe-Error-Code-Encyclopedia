MAINFRAME.ENC.DB(-519)

SQLCODE -519 PREPARE STATEMENT IDENTIFIES OPEN CURSOR STATEMENT

  • E · ERROR
  • CURSOR
  • Databases
Explanation
Cannot PREPARE the statement that is the SELECT statement of an open cursor.
Likely cause
PREPARE attempted while a cursor based on the same statement is open.
Programmer response
CLOSE the cursor first, then PREPARE.
Example
OPEN C1; PREPARE S1 FROM :SQL;  /* C1 based on S1 */
