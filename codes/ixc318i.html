MAINFRAME.ENC.MVS(IXC318I)

IXC318I COUPLING FACILITY EVENT

  • W · WARNING
  • SYSPLEX
  • Operating System and MVS
Explanation
A Coupling Facility structure encountered a problem — failure, rebuild, or connectivity issue.
Likely cause
CF lost connectivity, structure full, or duplexing partner failed.
Programmer response
Check CF status with D CF, review IXC messages for the structure, and rebuild if needed.
Example
IXC318I CONNECTIVITY TO COUPLING FACILITY CF01 LOST
